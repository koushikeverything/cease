# Amazon — Brand Registry infringement report

**Not a DMCA notice.** Amazon runs its own process with its own evidence
expectations, and submitting a § 512 notice here usually just slows things down.

Requires enrolment in Brand Registry, which the brand must do itself. CEASE can
prepare and track the application; it cannot substitute for it.
