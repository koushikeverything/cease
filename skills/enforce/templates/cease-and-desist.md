# Cease and desist — for attorney review

**This is escalation, never the opening move.** Send it after platform remedies
have been tried and failed, not before. Opening with a C&D against a large
infringer invites a declaratory-judgment action on their home turf, and against
a small one it usually achieves less than a payment-processor report.

**CEASE does not practise law.** This is a draft for a qualified attorney to
review, adjust and send. Do not send it unreviewed.
