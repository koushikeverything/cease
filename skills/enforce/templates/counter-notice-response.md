# Counter-notice response — decision memo

Under **17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3)** the infringer may file a counter-notice. The
service provider must then restore the material in **10 to 14 business days**
unless the rights holder files a court action seeking to restrain the infringing
activity and tells the provider so.

This template is a **decision memo for a human**, not a filing. The decision
is whether to litigate. That is not a decision CEASE makes.
