If the provider forwarded it today (Fri 2026-09-18), the window would be **Fri 2026-10-02 at the earliest, Thu 2026-10-08 at the outside** — worked example only; the real dates key off the forward date, not today, and are counted in US business days in your jurisdiction, not the local calendar.

Two things worth knowing:

- The counter-notice comes with the counter-notifier's **name, address, and consent to federal jurisdiction**. That's the practical point of it — it's how you'd sue them if you go that route.
